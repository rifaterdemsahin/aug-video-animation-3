Canva Video Editor

The multi-track timeline workspace organized into 3 sections: Pre-Prod (22 script Post-it notes), Prod (8s Google Flow video clips on tracks), and Post-Prod (VO audio mastering, Roger Rabbit cartoon stamp, 1080p60 export).

✨ Signature Gate: Sort footages into used asset and scrub timeline with shotlist in hand using the sliding Post-it note to catch any gaps before Post-Prod.

FlyWheelMVP

Google's AI filmmaking tool rendering high-resolution, cinematic 8-second motion graphic video slices at 15 credits per clip.

AI Director & Script Engine

Acts as the Video Director, turning raw classroom topic notes into the rigorous 22 scenes × 8s pacing framework with synchronized visual concepts and strict 18–22 word VO lines.
