⚡ The 8-Second Formula

8 seconds is the optimal attention window for social algorithms (YouTube, Shorts, LinkedIn, X) while maintaining deep technical substance.

🗣️ The 18–22 Word Pacing Rule

Natural speech rate is ~2.5 words/sec. An 8-second block comfortably holds 18–22 words with natural breathing room and zero audio rush.

🎬 Roger Rabbit Signature Style

Blend real terminal & IDE screen captures (127.0.0.1:3847) with cartoon characters and La Linea continuous line overlays via Google Flow.

The 3-Section Production Methodology

Standardized assembly flow across Canva, Google Flow, and local asset staging.

📝 1. Pre-Production Section (~35 min)

Set up the 14-tab Chrome cockpit, extract Skool courseware, run Gemini Director prompts, and place 22 color-coded Post-it script notes above Canva timeline tracks.

  • ✔ 14 orchestrated Chrome tabs staged
  • ✔ 22 scene script lines locked (~18-22 words)
  • ✔ Dual split-view simulation prepared
🎥 2. Production Section (~75 min)

Render 22 8s slices in Google Flow, upload to aug video 3, place clips on Canva timeline, and move footages one-by-one to used asset.

  • ✔ 22 Google Flow video slices generated
  • ✔ Master 8s placeholder bulk-paste
  • ✔ QC Gate: Zero-gap audit with shotlist in hand
🎙️ 3. Post-Production Section (~35 min)

Snap videos as background, record voice-over via 8s Teleprompter Studio, sync Roger Rabbit cartoon overlays to audio beats, and export 1080p 60fps MP4.

  • ✔ 8s Voice-Over rehearsal and recording
  • ✔ Roger Rabbit animation signature stamp
  • ✔ Multi-platform distribution & SRT sync
