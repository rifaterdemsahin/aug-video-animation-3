🧠 Review · Sanity Check

Does this pipeline design hold?

Review of the CODE production design (Capture → Organize → Distill → Express): 10 stages, 22 × 8s = 176s master, ~75 minutes of timed work. Grounded in the logged issues (Stage 1 stuck at 50, VO placed too late, cloned voice on fal.ai).

Verdict: The shape is sound — a short, gated flywheel with a hard 8-second beat and a quality gate before timeline lock. It will not hold at the printed times, and it will keep redoing work until voice and pacing sit earlier. Treat ~75 min as a best-case studio day, not a committed SLA. Stage 3 (Flow, 30 min / ~40% of timed work) and Stage 1 (untimed, capacity 50) are the real constraints.

✅ Pros

  • CODE maps cleanly to video. Capture raw classroom, Organize clips and prompts, Distill voice, Express distribution. Operators can find the current phase without a 20-item SOP.
  • Fixed 8s / 18–22 word contract. 22 scenes × 8s = 176s is a real production unit. Prompts, VO, and timeline snap to the same beat.
  • Two gates exist. Stage 1.5 (prompt/asset sanity) and Stage QC (shot list vs used assets) stop “generate 22 clips then discover a hole.”
  • Stage 3 is called out as the bulk. 15 + 15 min and ~330 Flow credits is honest: generation is the expensive step, not Canva paste.
  • Native distribution, not one export-everywhere file. YouTube chapters, LinkedIn, Skool pin — matches a flywheel instead of a dump to Drive.
  • Whiteboard lives on Feedback only. Detail pages stay operational; review does not compete with the board.

❌ Cons

  • Most 5-minute stages are fiction. Scripting 22 Flow prompts, QC of 22 clips, Canva paste, VO clone, and 3-platform publish rarely finish in 5 minutes each. Calendar math will slip.
  • Stage 1 has no time and a hard cap. “Stuck at 50” is a capacity bottleneck, not a setup task. Capture is unplanned work sitting in front of the flywheel.
  • Voice is too late. Stage 4.5 already flags “place earlier to prevent redo.” Generating 22 clips before VO lock means re-timing or re-rendering when lines change.
  • Tool chain is wide. Skool, Obsidian, Gemini, Flow, Canva, fal.ai, ElevenLabs, YouTube Studio. Handoffs are copy-paste and downloads, not a single project file.
  • Tool vs issue mismatch on VO. Distill lists fal.ai for rehearsal and ElevenLabs for clone, then an issue says “use cloned voice with fal.ai.” Operators will not know the source of truth.
  • QC after 30 min of generation is thin at 5 min. A real gap audit of 22 clips against a shot list needs more than a folder create + scrub.

⚠️ Risks

  • Redo loop from late VO. If VO/captions change after Flow render, you burn another ~330 credits and another 30 minutes. Highest cost risk in the design.
  • Flow credit and queue failure. Batch of 22 × ~15 credits is a single-vendor outage. No listed fallback generator or stills path.
  • Style drift across 22 clips. Roger Rabbit / isometric lock is prompt-only. Without a locked reference still per scene, QC will pass “a clip exists” and fail “the series matches.”
  • Capture starvation. If Skool intake stays stuck at 50, Organize has nothing honest to prompt. The flywheel idles while looking busy at Stage 2.
  • Optimistic Express. 1080p 60fps export + 22 YouTube chapters + LinkedIn + Skool pin in 5 minutes assumes templates already exist. First run will not.
  • Clone/legal and sync risk. Cloned voice + caption mismatch + speed-ramping clips to fit 8s can produce uncanny pacing and platform policy issues if the clone is not clearly owned.

🌱 Opportunities

  • Move VO lock before Flow. Freeze 22 × 18–22 word lines (and metronome) in Capture/Organize. Then generate picture to locked audio. Kills the 4.5 redo note.
  • One reference still + prompt pack. Stage 1.5 should output a style board and a 22-row prompt table, not only “placeholders queued.”
  • Treat Stage 1 as a backlog, not a stage with no clock. Cap weekly topics (the “50”) and pull one classroom item into the flywheel. Makes Capture measurable.
  • Credit buffer and a B-roll fallback. Budget 330 + 20% rerolls. Keep a Canva motion/placeholder path so QC can pass a draft without waiting on Flow.
  • Template the 5-minute stages. Canva 3-section file, YouTube chapter generator, Skool pin copy — then those times become real on run 3+, not run 1.
  • Pick one voice vendor. Either fal.ai clone or ElevenLabs, with captions from the same transcript. Remove the dual-tool contradiction.

Stage-by-stage sanity

Stage Claim Check Action
1 Skool Untimed capture Fail — stuck at 50, no WIP limit Timebox one classroom extract per video
1.5 Gate 5 min sanity + 22 placeholders Pass as a gate; time is tight Include style-lock stills, not only queue
2 Script 5 min for 22 prompts + VO lines Fail on time if starting from blank Reuse Gemini director session; freeze VO here
2.5 Dual 5 min split-screen Pass — cockpit, not content Keep; do not treat as a content stage
3 Flow 30 min / 330 credits Pass as the real work Add reroll buffer; this is the critical path
QC 5 min gap audit Fail if 22 clips + style check Shot list checkbox per scene; block Stage 4 until 22/22
4 Timeline 5 min paste + background Pass once the Canva template exists Never assemble before QC 22/22
4.5 VO 5 min, after picture Fail order — already logged as too late Move before Stage 3
VO Sync 10 min clone + retiming Fail if clips must be re-sped Clone to locked script; avoid stretching picture
5 Express 5 min three platforms Fail on first publish; pass with templates Chapter + description snippets generated in Stage 2
Do this next (smallest design change): Freeze the 22 VO lines in Stage 2, run Stage 4.5 rehearsal before Stage 3, keep QC as a hard 22/22 gate, and give Stage 1 a weekly WIP of one classroom topic. Leave CODE and the 8-second beat as they are.
